hint → #2

The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. Its tenants write the day first. People type what the hint tells them, the form reads it the tenant’s way, and day and month swap. The parser fix does not touch this: the form reads the date day first, as it should, after the hint has made people type it wrong. Fix the hint per tenant locale. 33 reports, all through the form, from 3 tenants on booking-v3.

shift → #3

These dates are not swapped. They move by one day, and the day depends on where the reader is: one meeting showed Tuesday in Lisbon and Monday in Toronto. A day/month parser cannot do that; a time zone or daylight-saving conversion can. The desk read them as a one-day move (desk-notes.json) but filed them under parser by tenant, so the parser fix would close them unfixed. They stay counted there and count here too. 5 reports:
